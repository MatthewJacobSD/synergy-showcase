01 — Brief

What we were asked to do

Quest 5 is the final reflection quest of the INT Synergy course. It asks each member to present a personal introduction that brings together cultural background, learning experiences, and individual growth throughout the seven-week programme.

This quest serves as both a personal summary and a way for the team to share what they have learned with each other and with the wider audience at the Synergy ShowDown.

02 — Approach

Our approach

Each member prepared their own personal introduction, reflecting on their journey through the course — from the first quest to the final showcase. We then combined our individual reflections into a single group presentation.
