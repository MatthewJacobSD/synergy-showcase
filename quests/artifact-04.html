Artifact 04

The Culture Games

Using Hofstede's cultural dimensions to understand how people work differently. This artifact compares home countries with the Netherlands on key dimensions and translates findings into actionable teamwork tips.

Quest 4 — Hofstede's Dimensions
Status In Progress
Members Matthew, Elina, Thijmen

What we were asked to do

Part 1 asked each member to choose one or two Hofstede cultural dimensions, compare their home country with the Netherlands using data, and write a short reflection on what the scores mean for how people approach work and collaboration.

Part 2 required writing one concrete, actionable teamwork tip — not generic advice — and a short micro-scenario: a brief, realistic situation that shows the tip in practice.

Our approach

Each member selected dimensions relevant to their own cultural background, researched the Hofstede scores, and wrote reflections comparing their home country with the Netherlands. We then shared our teamwork tips and scenarios in a combined presentation.

Content pending

The individual contributions for this artifact are being prepared. The PowerPoint presentation and detailed write-ups will be added once the team completes the quest work.
