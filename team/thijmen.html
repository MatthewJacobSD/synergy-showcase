Part 1 — My AI Usage Plan

How I use AI

I often use AI as a substitute to Google for research purposes. It summarizes a bunch of pages easily and when needed has the option to give sources too — it just speeds things up a lot. I also have used it in the past to program things without prior knowledge of a language, like before I started learning C or C++.

What responsible use looks like

Double check important information. For example, go to the actual sources and see if what the summary says is accurate. For coding related tasks, I always skim through the code and determine whether it is good code, and if I am unsure about it I ask for reasoning or fix it myself.

How I will avoid GiGo

  • Double check information
Part 2 — Prompt Scenario

Bottle cap identifier

Context

I have been collecting bottle caps for over 10 years and have collected over 300 unique ones, but I am starting to lose track of which ones I have and do not have, so I recently started working on a better way to catalogue and identify them.

Bad input Fails

How can I make a bottle cap identifier

Why it fails

Way too generic, there is 0 context behind it and no clear expectations.

Better input Clear expectations

I am making a bottle cap identifier, it will need to scan the caps, store their picture somewhere and be able to compare the scanned caps with newly scanned caps from anywhere to check whether the can is unique or not, what can I use to scan them, store them, compare them, run it 24/7 and access it from anywhere outside of my local network.

Why it works better

Has very clear instructions, expectations and context.

Verification checklist

Go to the Google/GitHub pages of the listed systems and check if this matches what was asked for and if I will be able to use it (no specific licencing or technical complications). If they do, go back and ask for more info on the specifics. If they do not, explain what is wrong with them and ask to find another way.
