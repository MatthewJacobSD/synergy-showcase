Artifact 03 · Quest 03

Lost in Translation, Found in Structure

Exploring Dutch grammar as a structure and comparing it across languages. This artifact examines how meaning changes when translated, and what structural patterns emerge across different language systems.

Quest 3 — Language & Grammar
Status In Progress
Members Matthew, Elina, Thijmen
01 02 03 04 05
01 — Brief

What we were asked to do

Part 1 asked each member to choose one Dutch grammar rule they found interesting or surprising, explain it clearly enough for someone who does not know Dutch to follow, and provide at least two examples including one original construction.

Part 2 required taking those sentences and expressing the same meaning in a third language — not Dutch or English — then noting two to four specific contrasts: what is different, what is similar, and what surprised you.

02 — Approach

Our approach

Each member chose a Dutch grammar rule to investigate, wrote examples, and then translated those examples into a third language from their own background. We then compared our findings in a group presentation.
