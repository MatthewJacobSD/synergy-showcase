Introduction

From quests to artifacts

Throughout the INT Synergy course, we worked through a series of activities and quests designed to develop our understanding through practical group work.

Each member contributed their own ideas and work to the activities. We then combined our individual contributions into group artifacts, allowing us to present the outcome of our work together.

For our final showcase, we chose to present five PowerPoint artifacts created throughout the course. This website brings those presentations together in one place and provides additional context about what each artifact represents, how it was created, and how our individual contributions came together.

The PowerPoints are the artifacts themselves; this website acts as the final layer that connects and presents them as one collection.

Challenges

Surpassing obstacles

Working together throughout the course was not always straightforward. Like any group project, we encountered challenges involving communication, coordination, and combining individual work into a consistent final result.

These challenges required us to adapt how we worked together and find ways to continue progressing despite differences in availability, communication, and individual approaches.

Rather than allowing these obstacles to prevent us from completing the project, we focused on bringing our separate contributions together and delivering the artifacts we had committed to producing.

The final website represents the result of that process: five artifacts brought together into one coherent showcase.

Collaboration

Built together

The final result is based on collaboration between all three group members. Although our responsibilities were divided, the artifacts were produced through the combination of our individual contributions.

Two members focused primarily on constructing the PowerPoint presentations, while the website was developed to provide a single place where all of the completed work could be presented and understood as part of the same project.

This division of responsibilities allowed each member to focus on a specific part of the final product while still contributing to the overall group outcome.

We would also like to thank our lecturer for providing guidance and support throughout the INT Synergy course.
